# FORBIDDEN CLAIMS

Visitor-facing demo LPs and outreach copy must NOT assert the terms below. They imply
medical/beauty efficacy or guaranteed results and create claim-safety / legal risk.

## Forbidden claim terms

- 改善 (improvement)
- 効果 (effect)
- 小顔 (small-face)
- 不眠 (insomnia)
- 自律神経 (autonomic nervous system)
- 妊活 (fertility)
- 脱毛 (hair removal)
- 治る (cured)
- 痛みが取れる (pain removed)
- 医療的効果 (medical effect)
- before/after
- fake testimonial
- guaranteed result
- 集客アップ保証 (guaranteed customer-acquisition increase)

## Contextual caution

These words may legitimately appear in **SOURCE materials** (the salon's own site, menus,
notes). That is fine as input. But **visitor-facing demo/outreach must OMIT or safely rewrite**
them: describe the **atmosphere and experience** (calm space, careful hands, time to unwind),
**not asserted effects or outcomes**.

Examples of safe rewrites:
- "小顔効果" → "丁寧なフェイシャルの時間"
- "不眠が改善" → "静かで落ち着ける空間"
- "guaranteed results" → "一人ひとりに合わせた施術"

## Enforcement

A **Python forbidden-claim scan** runs on the visitor-facing HTML before any send-readiness
verdict. Any hit blocks READY status until the term is removed or safely rewritten. This is a
code gate, not a judgment call — a single hit fails the scan.
