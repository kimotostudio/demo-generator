# SEND POLICY

## Manual send only

Outreach is **manual send only**. Yu sends every message by hand. The system never sends.
This stays in force until **explicitly upgraded by Yu via a phase gate** — a casual message
("you can send", "go ahead") does **NOT** upgrade it. Only a deliberate phase-gate decision does.

## Channels

- **Email and contact-form are preferred.**
- **No phone** under any circumstance.
- **No DM** unless Yu explicitly chooses DM for a specific lead.

## Caps and future auto-send

Send caps and any future auto-send capability are **gated and out of current scope**. They
unlock only through a Phase-2/3 gate plus the compliance build plus Yu's explicit approval.
Do not design or trigger sends against future caps in the current phase.

## Ledger state

`sent=0` for every lead until **Yu confirms** the manual send happened. See `LEDGER_POLICY.md`
for how `sent=1` is recorded.

## Deploy reminder

Deploy ≠ send. Deploying a demo (preview or public) does not contact anyone and does not
change send state.
